# Barometer

Barometer is a private anxiety log. It's switched off unless you've asked for it. It follows a common CBT (cognitive behavioural therapy) anxiety-tracking format and works best alongside a therapist. It isn't a substitute for care. If you're struggling right now, Samaritans are there day or night on 116 123 (free, UK).

Nobody else can see your readings, including people you share your calendar with.

## Taking a reading

Choose **Barometer → Take a reading**. Only the day and a rating from 0 (calm) to 10 (most anxious) are needed, so you can log one in a couple of taps and fill in the rest later. The day and time start at now. Change them to log something from earlier, or tick **I don't remember the time** if you're writing up a worry from another day.

The rest is optional:

- **Situation**: where you were and what was happening.
- **Thoughts**: what went through your mind.
- **Physical sensations**: breathing, heart, stomach, tension.
- **What did I do?** and **What did I say to myself?**
- **Second rating**: how anxious you feel a little later. Open the reading from the list to add it.

Deleting a reading removes it for good. There's no undo.

## Exporting for your therapist

At the bottom of the Barometer page, pick a date range (up to a year) and choose **Download PDF** or **Download CSV**. The PDF prints on A4 and is laid out like the paper form, titled "Anxiety log". The CSV opens in Excel or Numbers.

Untick any column you'd rather not share that week. The time and first rating are always included.
