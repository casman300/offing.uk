# Shipshape: household chores

Shipshape keeps track of the jobs that keep a home running: the bins, the hoovering, the monthly deep clean, the car tax. You tick them off when they're done and Shipshape works out when each one is due next.

Shipshape is still being rolled out, so it may not be switched on for your account yet.

Every chore repeats. Shipshape isn't a to-do list, so one-off jobs like "fix the shed door" don't belong here.

## Lists

Chores live on lists. A list is either **just yours**, or **shared with a crew** (your household, for example). Everyone in the crew sees a shared list's chores and can tick them off.

To start one, go to [Shipshape](/shipshape), choose **Lists**, give it a name and pick who it's shared with. To share chores you need to be in a crew first. You can start one on the [Crew](/crew) page, then invite your household by email: they get a link, and if they're new to Offing all they need to give is their name.

When you create a list, Shipshape offers some suggestions, all ticked: daily jobs, weekly cleaning, a monthly deep clean and yearly renewals like car tax and the MOT. Untick anything that doesn't fit. You can come back to them later with **Suggestions** on the list.

## How chores repeat

Each chore repeats in one of two ways:

- **From when it's done**: the clock restarts when you tick it. If you clean the oven a week late, the next one is due three months after you actually did it. This suits cleaning.
- **On set dates**: the chore stays on its pattern however early or late you are. Bins every Tuesday stay on Tuesdays. This suits bills and renewals.

A renewal can be left without a date if you don't know it yet. It sits under **Needs a date** and never shows as overdue. You can also ask for a **heads-up**, so car insurance appears on the board a few weeks before it renews, giving you time to shop around.

If you miss a chore, it shows once as overdue rather than piling up.

## The board

The [board](/shipshape) shows every chore you can see, sorted by when it's due: overdue, today, the next 7 days, later, and needs a date. Your [Dashboard](/dashboard) shows today's and overdue chores too, alongside birthdays coming up.

Tap the circle to tick a chore off. If you tapped by mistake, choose **Undo** in the next few seconds.

Use the **…** button to:

- say who did it, including someone without an account, like a child
- say when it was done (yesterday, or an earlier date)
- skip it, for example if you're away. A skip moves the date on but isn't counted as done.

## Who does what

On a shared list, a chore can be done by **anyone**, **one person**, or a **rota** that takes turns. The rota moves on each time the chore is done. If someone covers your turn, the rota still carries on in the same order, so nobody loses their place.

Anyone in the crew can tick any chore. If someone leaves the crew, their chores go back to "anyone" and they drop out of any rotas.

A crew's captains can make a list **captains only**, so only captains can add, change or delete its chores. Everyone can still tick them off.
