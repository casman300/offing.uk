# Signing in

Offing doesn't use passwords. There are two ways in.

## With a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN. No email is needed.

On the [sign-in page](/login), choose **Sign in with a passkey**. On many devices your passkey is also offered when you tap the email field.

To set one up, see [Passkeys](Passkeys.md).

## With an email link

1. Go to the [sign-in page](/login) and enter your email address.
2. We email you a one-time sign-in link.
3. Click the link. It opens a page showing which account it signs in to. Press **Continue** and you're in.

If the email shown isn't yours, close the page. Nothing happens unless you press Continue.

Each link works once and expires soon after it's sent. If yours has expired, request a new one from the sign-in page.

Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder, then ask for a new link.

Email links always work, even after you've set up a passkey. If you lose your phone, you can still get back in with your email.

## Where you're signed in

**Settings → Sign-in & devices** lists every browser and device signed in to your account, such as "Chrome on Windows" or "Safari on iPhone". Each shows when it signed in, when it was last used and the internet address it was last used from. Each browser is listed separately, even two on the same computer.

If you see one you don't recognise, or you've signed in on someone else's computer, choose **Sign out** next to it. It's signed out straight away and needs an email link or passkey to get back in. **Sign out everywhere else** does the same for every browser but the one you're using.

If a browser you know changes name or address in the list without you signing in again, someone may have copied its sign-in. Sign it out, then sign in again on that device.

You stay signed in on each device for 90 days after you last used it.

## Changing your email

Your email address is how you sign in, so changing it takes two steps:

1. In **Settings → Account**, enter your new address and save.
2. We email a confirmation link to the new address. Your email only changes when you click it (within 24 hours). Until then, keep signing in with your current address. You can cancel the change from the same page.

Once it's changed, we email your old address to let you know. If you didn't make the change, the **This wasn't me** button in that email puts your old address back for up to 7 days. It also signs out every device and removes anything added since the change: passkeys, calendar feed links, notification devices and AI connections. Then sign in again with an email link.
