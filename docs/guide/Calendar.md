# Your calendar

**Calendar** in the menu is your own diary: everything coming up, grouped by day, with today at the top.

## Adding an event

Choose **Add event**, give it a title and pick when it starts and ends. Moving the start keeps the length the same, so a one-hour event stays one hour. Tick **All day** for things like holidays or a day off; an all-day event can run over several days.

Times are in your own timezone (set in **Settings → Profile**). If an event is somewhere else, such as a dinner in Paris, choose **Change** and pick that timezone. You'll see the time in yours, with the local time underneath ("20:00 in Europe/Paris"). All-day events stay on the same dates wherever you are.

Location and notes are optional.

Events happen once, for now. Repeating events are coming.

## Private and free

Two separate settings change what other people learn about an event:

- **Private**: only you see what it is. Once you share your calendar, a partner sees a private event as a plain "Busy" block (or not at all if it's also marked free), and nobody else sees it.
- **Show me as free**: the event doesn't block your time, for things that don't stop you doing something else.

Sharing isn't switched on yet, so for now both settings only affect your own calendar feed: private events are marked private there, and free events don't show you as busy.

## Changing and deleting

Tap an event to edit it. To delete it, use the bin icon on the edit page. You can **Undo** straight away, or restore it later from **Recently deleted** at the bottom of the calendar.

## In your other calendar apps

Your events go out on your [calendar feed](Calendar-feeds.md) along with birthdays, so they show up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook too.
