# Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in to Offing with Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN, instead of waiting for an email link. It's stored by your device or password manager (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, 1Password and others), and it only works on Offing, so it can't be phished.

## Setting one up

The first time you sign in with an email link on a device, Offing asks whether you'd like to use Face ID next time. Choose **Set up a passkey** and follow your device's prompt. If you choose **Not now**, Offing won't ask again on that device for 30 days.

You can also add one at any time from **Settings → Passkeys**.

Passkeys saved to iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager sync to your other devices signed in to the same account, so one passkey often covers your phone and your laptop.

## Managing passkeys

**Settings → Passkeys** lists your passkeys and when each was last used. You can rename them (for example, "Work laptop") or remove them.

Whenever a passkey is added to your account, we email you. If you get that email and it wasn't you, remove the passkey straight away.

## If you lose your device

Email sign-in links always work, so you can't get locked out by losing a passkey. Sign in with an email link, then remove the lost device's passkey from **Settings → Passkeys**.
