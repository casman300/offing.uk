# Offing

**What's in the offing.** A shared calendar for the people in your life.

Most calendars can share your diary with the people you live with. Offing is built around the rest of your circle as well:

- **Partners** see everything, apart from events you mark private.
- **Friends** see only the events they're tagged in.
- **Clubs and groups** (the D&D club, the craft group) get a calendar feed they can subscribe to, with no account needed.

Offing is in early development and features arrive gradually, so some of what's described here may not be switched on for you yet.

## What we promise

- **No passwords, ever.** You sign in with a passkey (Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN) or a link sent to your email.
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.**
- **Open source.** All of the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/casman300/offing.uk), under the AGPL-3.0 licence.
